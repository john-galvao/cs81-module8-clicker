# Cookie Clicker Reflection

## What helped you understand DOM interaction best?
Updating the counter and message on every click helped me understand DOM interaction the most.

## How did you choose your milestone messages?
I chose the milestone messages based on the suggested milestones in the assignment. Each message reflects increasing progress and gives feedback to the user as the click count grows.

## What challenge or bug surprised you?
One small challenge was making sure the milestone messages triggered only at the exact click counts. This helped me understand how strict equality checks work inside conditional statements.

## What personal twist did you add?
I added a cookie emoji as the button and changed the background color at certain milestones to make progress more visible.

## What real-world app uses this kind of interaction?
Many games and apps use this kind of interaction, such as clicker games, fitness apps that track steps, or social media apps that update likes and counters when users interact.
